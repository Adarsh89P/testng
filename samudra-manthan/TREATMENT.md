# समुद्र मंथन: चौदह रत्न (Samudra Manthan: the 14 ratnas)

Style: Shadow Puppetry (hide puppets, oil-lamp light). 1920x1080, 24 fps, about 60 s. Hindi voice (Kokoro hm_omega, offline), Hindi burned-in captions + .srt, generated percussion/flute score.

## Three structures
1. **The churn as a journey**: gods and demons pull, things rise in order. Linear, the cliché.
2. **The lamp count**: each ratna lights one more lamp on the cloth; the screen blooms from dark to bright, and the last lamp is the poison turning it dark again. Uses the style's native move ("the light is the world").
3. **The rope's-eye view**: camera stays on Vasuki's coils and only glimpses what rises.

**Chosen: 2 (lamp count).** The film is a list that turns: fourteen things come out, but the first one is poison. The cloth starts lit by one lamp; the poison darkens it; then each ratna adds a lamp until the amrit-kalash. Counting = locked wide shot, one percussion hit per ratna.

## Logline
Before the nectar came, the ocean gave up thirteen other things, and the first of them was poison.

## Arc
Setup (mountain, serpent, two sides pull) -> Turn (Halahala darkens the cloth; Shiva drinks) -> Ending (lamps lit one by one to Dhanvantari with the amrit; Mohini, the lamp-carried-away fade).

## Sources (lists differ between texts, the film says so)
Bhagavata Purana 8.6-8.9; Vishnu Purana 1.9; Mahabharata Adi Parva 18. The film uses the popular fourteen: Halahala, Kamadhenu, Uchchaihshravas, Airavata, Kaustubha, Kalpavriksha/Parijata, Rambha (Apsara), Lakshmi, Varuni, Chandra, Panchajanya, Sharanga bow, Dhanvantari, Amrit. (Some texts add Jyestha/Alakshmi, Parijata separately from Kalpavriksha, or drop the bow.)

8. अंत में धन्वंतरि प्रकट हुए, हाथ में अमृत का कलश लिए।
9. पुराणों में सूची थोड़ी बदलती है, पर कथा वही है। अमृत से पहले, विष पीना पड़ा।

## Shots (reason in brackets)
1. 0-6 s Audience seat -> push through frame onto cloth (entering the tale), one lamp.
2. 6-12 s Lateral pan along the rope (extent of the pull); Kurma piece under the mountain.
3. 12-20 s Close on the carved poison piece; lamp snuffs, crash zoom on the gong; Shiva piece swallows (the turn, near-silence).
4. 20-48 s **Locked wide**, 11 ratnas appear one by one, each new lamp flares (counting). Card plaque names each.
5. 48-56 s Tilt up to Dhanvantari with the kalash, all lamps lit, cloth bleached.
6. 56-60 s Pull back into the dark room; lamp carried away, cloth darkens (ending).

## Sound
Barrel drum heartbeat under dread; one gong per decisive hit; small gong tick per ratna; bamboo flute for quiet; fiddle (plain pentatonic, ku-yin for the poison); silence at the poison and just before Dhanvantari. Voice ducks the bed ~12 dB.
