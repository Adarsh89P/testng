# प्रह्लाद — अटूट भक्ति के रहस्य (10 Shorts)

Source: Vishnu Purana, Amsha 1, Adhyaya 16–20 (Prahlada charita).
Each script starts with "क्या आपको पता है…", ≈ 120–150 words (55–65 s). Pause ~1 s at (रुकें).

| # | Title | Core |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | दैत्यराज का पुत्र, विष्णु का भक्त | Hiranyakashipu rules the three worlds; his son Prahlada is devoted to Vishnu |
| 2 | गुरुकुल का उत्तर | "The best thing I learned" — praise of Vishnu before his father |
| 3 | शस्त्र और सर्प | weapons fail; serpents' fangs break |
| 4 | दिग्गजों के दाँत | the elephants of the quarters — their tusks break |
| 5 | अग्नि जो जला न सकी | the fire feels like cool lotus petals; Holika is not in the VP version |
| 6 | पुरोहितों की कृत्या | the priests' krtya turns back on them; Prahlada revives them |
| 7 | विष, ऊँचाई और माया | poison digested; Earth catches him; Shambara's illusions; the wind |
| 8 | दैत्य बालकों को उपदेश | Prahlada teaches the daitya boys: no enmity, Vishnu is in all |
| 9 | समुद्र की गहराई में | bound with naga-pasha, thrown into the sea under mountains; Vishnu appears |
| 10 | प्रह्लाद का वरदान | unwavering devotion, and forgiveness for his father; Narasimha (mentioned) |

Verify before recording: the order of the trials, the Holika claim (ep 5), the krtya episode (ep 6),
and the boons (ep 10) against a Vishnu Purana translation (e.g. Gita Press, or H. H. Wilson 1840).
