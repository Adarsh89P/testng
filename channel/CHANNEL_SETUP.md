## 3. Rules for "secrets" (protects you and the channel)

- "Rahasya" means hidden meaning, not conspiracy. Explain what the text says and what the symbol means.
- Cite the source in every description (Bhagavata Purana 8th canto, Valmiki Ramayana, Rigveda mandala and sukta). Say "कहा जाता है" or "कुछ कथाओं के अनुसार" where versions differ.
- No invented miracles, no false science claims, no mocking other faiths. This also keeps you inside YouTube policy.
- Titles must match the video. Curiosity is fine, lies are not.

## 5. Findability checklist (the part that gets you found)

1. **Keywords first in the title.** Formula: `<Topic in Hindi>: <question or hook> | <Series>`. Example: `नीलकंठ: शिव का गला नीला क्यों हुआ? | शिव के 108 नाम`.
2. **First 2 lines of the description** hold the main keywords, in English and Hindi. YouTube shows about 150 characters in search.
3. **Research titles with search suggestions.** Type your topic into YouTube search and use the suggested phrases viewers actually type, in Hindi and in Roman letters (Hinglish).
4. **Upload captions in Hindi, English and Bengali** (`.srt`). Captions are indexed text and open the video to non-Hindi viewers.
5. **Chapters** in the description for videos over 3 minutes.
6. **3 hashtags max**, for example `#DharmaRahasya #ShivPuran #Sanatan`.
7. **Playlists** per pillar, with the series name in the playlist title and description.
8. **Thumbnails:** big Devanagari word, one focal image, same colour style. 1280x720.
9. **Pinned comment** with the source and the next video.
10. **End screen** pointing to the next video in the series.
11. **Consistency:** a fixed rhythm (for example 2 Shorts and 1 long video a week) beats occasional bursts.
12. **Shorts feed the long videos:** end each Short with "पूरा वीडियो चैनल पर", as in your current scripts.

## 7. Launch plan

- **Before going public:** profile photo, banner, watermark, description, 3 to 5 videos uploaded.
- **Week 1:** upload Neelkanth and Markandeya (long and short), set a trailer, create the playlists.
- **Weeks 2 to 4:** 1 long video and 2 Shorts per week across two pillars, so you learn which one works.
- **Review monthly** in YouTube Analytics: which titles got clicks, where viewers left, which pillar to grow.

## 8. Before monetizing

Check the current YouTube Partner Program thresholds, the voice-over licence terms of your ElevenLabs plan, and YouTube's rules on altered or synthetic content and on repetitive content. Keep each video's story and visuals distinct.
