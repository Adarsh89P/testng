# Channel setup — end to end

A Hindi YouTube channel of **oil-painted, scripture-sourced stories from Sanatan Dharma**: Puranas, Ramayana,
Mahabharata, Upanishads, festivals, symbols, avatars and tirthas. Every video opens with **"क्या आपको पता है…"**
and names its source.

## 2. Create the channel (YouTube Studio)

1. Create it as a **Brand Account** (Google account → YouTube → *Create a channel* → *Use a custom name*),
   so you can add managers later without sharing your password.
2. **Handle**: set it in *Customization → Basic info → Handle*.
3. Turn on **2-Step Verification** on the Google account (required for monetization).
4. Verify your phone number in *Settings → Channel → Feature eligibility* to unlock **custom thumbnails,
   videos longer than 15 minutes and live streaming**.
5. *Settings → Channel → Basic info*: Country **India**; keywords (below).
6. *Settings → Channel → Advanced*: Audience **"No, set this channel as not made for kids"**
   (mythology for a general audience; "made for kids" switches off comments and notifications).
7. *Settings → Upload defaults* (saves typing every upload):
   - Title: `| ग्रंथ रहस्य #shorts`
   - Description: the template in §5
   - Category: **Education**
   - Video language: **Hindi**; caption certification: none
   - License: Standard YouTube License; Comments: **On, hold potentially inappropriate comments for review**
8. **AI disclosure** ("Altered or synthetic content" toggle on each upload): YouTube does **not** require it for
   clearly animated or unrealistic content, which these painted videos are. Leave it **No**, and state the
   method honestly in the description ("चित्र: कोड से बनी तैलचित्र शैली · वाचन: AI स्वर").

**Channel keywords** (Settings → Basic info):

## 9. Production workflow (per Short)

1. **Topic + script** — I write ≈140 words, starting "क्या आपको पता है…", with the exact source. You check the source.
2. **Voice** — record in ElevenLabs (same voice every time); leave short pauses between sentences.
3. **Send me the MP3** — I time every line to it, build the oil-painted video, soundtrack and Hindi subtitles
   (≈1 hour render).
4. **Upload kit** — I give you title, description, hashtags and pinned comment.
5. **Batch:** script 4 at once, record 4 at once — one week of Shorts per session.

## 10. Growth habits

- The **first 2 seconds** carry the hook line *and* a striking image; never start on a title card alone.
- End every Short with a reason to watch the next part ("भाग 2 में…").
- Reply to comments in the first hour; pin a question on every Short.
- Use *Community posts*: a poll ("अगली कथा: ध्रुव या प्रह्लाद?") each week.
- Never claim one version is "the only truth" — present it as "विष्णु पुराण के अनुसार"; it keeps comments
  respectful and avoids misinformation reports.

## 11. Monetization — current rules and the 2027 change

**Now** (YouTube Partner Program):
- *Fan funding tier:* 500 subscribers + 3 public uploads in 90 days + 3,000 watch hours (12 months) **or**
  3 M Shorts views (90 days).
- *Ads tier:* 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours (12 months) **or** 10 M Shorts views (90 days).

**From 1 February 2027** (announced by YouTube on 10 Aug 2026): new applicants for ads/Premium need
**8,000 watch hours or 20 M Shorts views**; fan-funding thresholds stay the same. → This is why the plan includes
long videos: Shorts-feed views do not count toward watch hours.

Also required: 2-Step Verification, advanced features, no active strikes, one AdSense account.

**Originality:** YouTube does not pay for mass-produced, repetitive content. Keep every Short visually distinct
(new scenes and characters each time), keep scripts original, and keep the human editorial voice in titles,
pinned comments and community posts.
