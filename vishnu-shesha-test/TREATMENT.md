# Vishnu on Shesha Naag — Shadow Puppetry test frame

A single style frame (not a film yet), to judge whether Shadow Puppetry carries Puranic imagery.

## The image
Anantashayana on the Kshira Sagara. Mid-show: the cloth is lit by three oil lamps,
the ocean of milk is a band of carved wave strips along the bottom, and the great
serpent Shesha lies coiled on it. His hoods rise and fan out over the god's head
like a carved canopy. Vishnu reclines on the coils in profile, four-armed: conch
and chakra raised in the upper hands, a mace resting, a lotus held to the chest.
The chakra is the brightest thing on the cloth: a wheel cut almost entirely away
so the lamp burns through it.

## Three candidate compositions
1. **Wide, audience seat**: whole stage, lacquer frame and audience heads; the
   serpent bed small at centre. Says "this is a show", but loses carving detail.
2. **Locked medium on the cloth** (chosen): serpent bed fills the middle third,
   hoods to the upper third, ocean strip below, empty lit cloth either side.
   Carving is readable and the frame still breathes.
3. **Close on the hoods and chakra**: craft as meaning, but loses the reclining
   pose that makes the image recognisable.

Chosen: 2, with the stage frame's inner edge just visible at the sides.

## Dyes
Indigo (Vishnu's skin), saffron-gold (pitambara and crown), malachite / jade
(Shesha), vermilion (accents, lotus), raw hide and ink. Sympathetic faces are
open-cut. Shesha's hoods carry crescent-scale rows and diamond lattice.

# Same scene, Impasto Oil Painting (second test frame)

Same image, read through thick palette-knife paint instead of lamp-lit hide.

## Value plan (three values first)
- **Dark**: deep night-blue sky at the top, cool violet shadow in the ocean troughs.
- **Mid**: Shesha's jade-and-viridian coils and hoods, Vishnu's blue body.
- **Light**: a warm golden halo behind Vishnu's head and the chakra; the milk ocean's
  cream crests; the yellow pitambara.

## Candidate compositions
1. **Wide plate, low horizon**: the whole bed small in a vast sky. Calm, but the god is tiny.
2. **Locked medium, centred bed** (chosen): same framing as the shadow-puppet test so the
   two can be compared side by side.
3. **Push-in on face and hoods**: strongest painting, but loses the reclining pose.

## Colour
Warm light against cool shadow: a cobalt-to-violet sky warming to peach and gold at the
halo; cool cream/lilac milk ocean with warm crests; the purest chroma reserved for
Vishnu (ultramarine skin, cadmium-yellow silk, gold crown) and the burning chakra.
