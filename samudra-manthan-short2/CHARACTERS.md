# Character bible — समुद्र मंथन के रहस्य

Every character is defined once in `chars.js` (shapes + colour keys). `puppet.js` renders it as carved,
dyed hide; `oil.js` renders the same shapes as palette-knife paint. Reuse these files for every new Short,
so each character always looks the same.

| Character | Look (both styles) | Props |
|---|---|---|
| **दुर्वासा Durvasa** | ash-pale skin, tall matted jata tied with rudraksha, grey beard, three ash lines (tripundra) on the forehead, saffron dhoti and shawl, fierce brow | staff, rudraksha mala |
| **विद्याधरी Vidyadhari** | flying pose, gold blouse, red brocade skirt, jade scarf arching over her head, flowers in her bun | the divine garland |
| **इंद्र Indra** | golden-fair skin, very tall gold crown, red armour with gold collar, moustache, rides Airavata | the vajra |
| **ऐरावत Airavata** | white elephant, **four tusks**, red caparison with gold border and tassels, gold forehead ornament | — |
| **विष्णु Vishnu** | blue skin, four arms, tall gold kirita, yellow silk, red vanamala, sacred thread | chakra, conch, gada, lotus |
| **शिव Shiva** | ash-blue skin, jata with the crescent moon and the Ganga, third eye, serpent at the throat, tiger skin | trident with damaru |
| **ब्रह्मा Brahma** | three faces visible (of four), white beards, golden skin, red dhoti, saffron upper cloth | the Vedas, kamandalu |
| **देवता Devas** | open-cut faces (sympathetic), small gold crowns, red/orange and gold | — |
| **दैत्य Daityas** | solid dark-teal faces, horns, fangs, red spiky crown, teal and jade | spear |
