# ध्रुव — अटल तारे का रहस्य (10 Shorts)

Source: Vishnu Purana, Amsha 1, Adhyaya 11–12 (Dhruva charita); lineage to Prithu from Adhyaya 13.
Each script starts with "क्या आपको पता है…", ≈ 120–150 words (45–60 s). Pause ~1 s at (रुकें).

| # | Title | Core |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | आकाश का अटल तारा | the pole star was once a five-year-old boy; Uttanapada, Suruchi/Uttama, Suniti/Dhruva |
| 2 | पिता की गोद | Suruchi stops Dhruva from sitting on his father's lap |
| 3 | माँ सुनीति की सीख | Suniti teaches virtue, not hatred; Dhruva vows to win the highest place |
| 4 | नारद नहीं, सप्तर्षि? | in the VP the seven sages guide him (Narada is the Bhagavata version) |
| 5 | मधुवन की तपस्या | penance at Madhuvana on the Yamuna (later Mathura); the gods grow anxious |
| 6 | माँ की माया | a Kushmanda takes the form of his weeping mother |
| 7 | राक्षसों का भय | terrifying illusions fail; the gods go to Vishnu, who reassures them |
| 8 | शंख का स्पर्श | Vishnu appears; touches Dhruva's cheek with his conch; the stuti flows |
| 9 | पिछले जन्म का रहस्य | in his past life a Brahmin who wished to be a prince |
| 10 | ध्रुव पद | the place above all stars; Suniti a star beside him; lineage to Prithu (next series) |

Notes before recording: the mantra line in Part 5 is shortened for narration; check any name you want to stress against your own copy of the text.
