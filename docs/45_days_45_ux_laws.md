# 45 Days · 45 UX Laws (through live apps)

Series format (same as Day 1):
`DAY xx/45` badge + 45-dot progress bar on top · hook "UX designer ho? ___ kya hai?" · story with a live app ·
App A | App B split · LAW card (dot-formed title + definition + source) · DON'T | DO takeaway · "kal · Day xx+1: ___" teaser.
Voice: bf_emma speaking Hindi, no background audio, big English on-screen text.

App examples describe UI patterns these apps visibly use; the reels show generic dotted mock-ups (no logos/screenshots).

| Day | Law / principle | Live-app example | One-line idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Labor Illusion | Zomato live order tracking | Seeing the work makes waiting feel shorter |
| 02 | Hick's Law | Swiggy / Zomato filter chips | Fewer choices at a time = faster decisions |
| 03 | Fitts's Law | Zomato sticky "View Cart" bar | Big + near targets are faster to tap |
| 04 | Jakob's Law | Flipkart vs Amazon layouts | Users expect your app to work like the apps they already use |
| 05 | Miller's Law / Chunking | OTP boxes, Aadhaar 4-4-4 format | We hold ~7±2 items; chunk long info |
| 06 | Zeigarnik Effect | LinkedIn profile-completion meter | Unfinished tasks stay on our mind |
| 07 | Goal-Gradient Effect | Duolingo daily XP progress | Effort increases as the goal gets closer |
| 08 | Peak-End Rule | GPay / PhonePe payment-success animation | People judge an experience by its peak and its end |
| 09 | Von Restorff Effect | "Most popular" highlighted plan on pricing pages | The item that stands out gets remembered |
| 10 | Serial Position Effect | Instagram bottom bar (Home first, Profile last) | First and last items are remembered best |
| 11 | Aesthetic-Usability Effect | CRED | Beautiful UI is perceived as easier to use |
| 12 | Doherty Threshold | Instagram like turns red instantly | Responses under ~400 ms keep users engaged |
| 13 | Tesler's Law | UPI scan-and-pay | Complexity can't vanish; the system should absorb it |
| 14 | Postel's Law | Phone fields accepting +91 / spaces | Be liberal in what you accept from users |
| 15 | Law of Proximity | Amazon product page (price + offers grouped) | Things close together feel related |
| 16 | Law of Common Region | Swiggy restaurant cards | A shared boundary groups content |
| 17 | Law of Similarity | Instagram story rings | Similar-looking things feel related |
| 18 | Uniform Connectedness | Order-status stepper line | Connected elements read as one flow |
| 19 | Law of Prägnanz | WhatsApp's simple icons | We see the simplest possible shape |
| 20 | Occam's Razor | Google Search home page | Remove everything that isn't needed |
| 21 | Pareto Principle | WhatsApp chats front and centre | 20% of features drive 80% of use |
| 22 | Parkinson's Law | Saved addresses / autofill at checkout | Tasks expand to fill time; shorten them |
| 23 | Cognitive Load | GPay home: big Pay actions | Less to think about = fewer mistakes |
| 24 | Choice Overload | Netflix "Top 10" row | Too many options paralyse; curate |
| 25 | Mental Models | Cart icon in shopping apps | Design to match what users already believe |
| 26 | Banner Blindness | Promo carousels people skip | Anything that looks like an ad gets ignored |
| 27 | Scarcity | Amazon "Only 2 left in stock" | Limited things feel more valuable |
| 28 | Social Proof | Zomato ratings + review counts | We follow what others do |
| 29 | Anchoring | Myntra strike-through MRP | The first number frames the rest |
| 30 | Loss Aversion | Snapchat streaks | Losing hurts more than gaining feels good |
| 31 | Default Effect | Last-used UPI pre-selected at checkout | Most people keep the default |
| 32 | Endowment Effect | Spotify Premium free trial | Once it feels like ours, we don't want to give it up |
| 33 | Reciprocity | Canva free templates | Give value first, users give back |
| 34 | Commitment & Consistency | Duolingo streaks | Small commitments lead to bigger ones |
| 35 | Variable Reward | Instagram pull-to-refresh | Unpredictable rewards keep us checking |
| 36 | Progressive Disclosure | Google Maps step details on demand | Show the basics, reveal more when needed |
| 37 | Visibility of System Status | WhatsApp ticks (sent/delivered/read) | Always tell users what's happening |
| 38 | Recognition over Recall | Zomato veg / non-veg symbol | Let users recognise, not remember |
| 39 | Error Prevention | UPI shows receiver's name before paying | Stop mistakes before they happen |
| 40 | Consistency & Standards | Same icons/gestures across Google apps | Same thing should look and work the same |
| 41 | Affordances & Signifiers | Blinkit "ADD" button | It should look like what it does |
| 42 | Microinteractions | Instagram double-tap heart | Tiny animations give feedback and delight |
| 43 | Hook Model | Instagram notification → open → reward | Trigger, action, reward, investment |
| 44 | Empty States | Empty cart / empty inbox screens | An empty screen should guide the next step |
| 45 | Good Friction | UPI PIN before payment | Some friction protects users |
