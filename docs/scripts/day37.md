## Roman (same words)
```
Do neeli lines, aur kitni dostiyaan toot gayi. Day thirty-seven mein, aaj in ticks ka poora raaz.
Aapne message bheja. Ek grey tick, yaani bhej diya. Do grey tick, yaani pahunch gaya. Aur do neele tick, yaani padh liya. Aur phir, reply nahi aaya.
Ab socho, agar ye ticks hote hi nahi. Message gaya ya nahi, phone band hai ya nahi, aap bas andaaza lagaate rehte.
Ise kehte hain, Visibility of System Status. System mein kya chal raha hai, user ko hamesha, saaf aur turant pata hona chahiye.
Order tracking mein rider do minute door, download ki progress bar, UPI mein processing, sab yahi law hai.
Jab sab dikhta hai, toh intezaar bhi chhota lagta hai. Aur jab kuch nahi dikhta, toh paanch second bhi, paanch minute lagte hain.
Toh designers, user ko kabhi andhere mein mat chhodo. Har tap ke baad, kuch toh jawaab do. Ek tick, ek spinner, ya bas, ho gaya.
Aap blue tick on rakhte ho, ya off? Comment mein on ya off likho. Aur kal, ek chhota sa hara-laal nishaan, jo bina padhe khaana chunwa deta hai.
```
