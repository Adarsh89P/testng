Day twenty-nine. Aaj ek chhota sa price game khelte hain.
Ek saadi si T-shirt, nau sau ninyaanve rupaye. Sasti lagi, ya mehngi? Thodi mehngi na?
Ab wahi T-shirt, Myntra par. Upar kata hua, do hazaar nau sau ninyaanve, aur neeche, nau sau ninyaanve. Sixty-six percent off. Ab? Ekdum deal lagi na?
Ise kehte hain, Anchoring. Jo number hum sabse pehle dekhte hain, baaki sab usi se taulte hain. Woh pehla number, dimaag ka langar ban jaata hai.
Restaurant ke menu mein bhi, aksar ek bahut mehngi dish hoti hai. Use koi kam hi order karta hai, par uske bagal mein, baaki dishes sasti lagne lagti hain.
Par dhyaan rahe. Agar MRP jaanboojhkar badhaakar likhi ho, toh ye trick nahi, dhokha hai. Aur user ye baat yaad rakhta hai.
Toh jab bhi price dikhao, socho, user sabse pehle kaun sa number dekhega. Aur woh number, hamesha sachcha hona chahiye.
Aapki sabse badi sale waali khareedaari kaun si thi? Comment mein batao. Kal milte hain, Loss Aversion ke saath.
