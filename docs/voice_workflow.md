# Voice-over workflow (standard from Day 04 on)

All 45-day reels use the ElevenLabs voice **Tisha K – Expressive, Lively Vlogger**, generated by Rakhi on her own
ElevenLabs account (this environment cannot reach ElevenLabs).

1. Each day's narration lives in `docs/scripts/dayNN.md`: one line per scene, `<break time="1.5s" />` between lines.
2. Generate it in ElevenLabs and save it as `assets/voice/dayNN/full.mp3` (gitignored media; upload it to the session).
3. `python3 figma/dayNN_*.py output/reels/dayNN.mp4`: if the mp3 exists it is split at the pauses
   (`engine.split_audio(path, "auto", n=len(SCENES))`) and each scene stretches to fit its line; otherwise an hf_beta
   TTS preview is rendered. For audio without clear pauses, pass explicit cut times (see `day04_jakobs_law.VO_CUTS`).
